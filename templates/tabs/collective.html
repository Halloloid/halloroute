Parallel Mode

Fan-out → Aggregate → Critique → Synthesize. Multiple specialist agents work in parallel, their outputs are aggregated, critiqued, and synthesized into a final response.

  • Planner: requirements, constraints, plan
  • Explorer: files, symbols, patterns
  • Architect: design, trade-offs, edges
  • Implementer: concrete change proposal
  • Tester: tests & failure scenarios
  • Reviewer: security & reliability audit
⟳

GAN Loop Mode

Planner → Generator ⇄ Evaluator (iterative). A single controlled loop where the Generator proposes implementations and the Evaluator scores them, iterating until a quality threshold is met.
